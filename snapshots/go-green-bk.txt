Craft Nights (Domino Park)
Craft Nights (Domino Park)
Monday, September 14th at 6:00 PM - 9:00 PM
Free
&laquo; Molière’s Don Juan (Prospect Park)
Molière’s Don Juan (Prospect Park) &raquo;
Get creative in community with our guided art-making workshops.
Join us for Craft Nights, happening twice a month on the 2nd and last Monday – a creative space to unwind, explore, and make something with your hands.
We’ll alternate between two partners: Nelise Bey’s natural dye workshops, where you can experiment with indigo and other plant-based colors, and Kimberly Lewis’s guided artmaking sessions. Kimberly’s approach invites you to reconnect with yourself through creating, offering hands-on projects that encourage experimentation, storytelling, and letting go of perfection.
.
THE SCHEDULE
MONDAY, JUNE 15: Natural Dye with Nelise Bey
MONDAY, JUNE 29: Sun Prints with Kimberly Lewis . Create one-of-a-kind prints using sunlight, shadows, and real natural materials collected from the park. Leaves, flowers, and found textures are arranged on light-sensitive paper to reveal soft silhouettes shaped by the sun.
MONDAY, JULY 13 : Natural Dye with Nelise Bey
MONDAY, JULY 27 : Summer Window Catcher with Kimberly Lewis. Create a glowing window hanging using recycled materials, tissue paper, and other translucent materials. Layer color and texture in response to the summer sunlight, sky, and the shifting moods of summer. Each participant will walk away with a personal light and color collage.
MONDAY, AUGUST 10: Felt Park Pendants with Kimberly Lewis . A one-night summer camp–style making experience at Domino Park. Personalize your own felt pendant using felt shapes, letter stickers, glue, and simple stitch details to build a small wearable or hangable keepsake.
MONDAY, AUGUST 24: Natural Dye with Nelise Bey
MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 14 : Natural Dye with Nelise Bey
MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 28: River Ink Flow Prints with Kimberly Lewis . Explore the movement of color through fluid ink painting inspired by the East River. No painting experience is required, participants will tilt, blow, and layer inks, allowing water and pigment to move like natural currents.
.
ABOUT OUR PARTNERS
NELISE BEY : Nelise is a textile artist, natural dyer, and printmaker living and creating in Brooklyn, NY. Her aesthetic is colorful and pattern-filled, a play on her Caribbean roots. Nelise works mostly with silks and other natural fibers, dyeing and silkscreening them. Her work is tied to the earth, and inspiration often calls when she’s outdoors hiking or foraging in Brooklyn and New York State.
KIMBERLY LEWIS : Kimberly Lewis is a Brooklyn-based artist, designer, and creative disruptor who believes perfection is boring and mess is magic. Molded by her creative studies at Pratt and FIT, she has spent 20+ years bending the edges of what creativity should look like, guiding both adults and kids to unlearn, reimagine, and express themselves freely through art, story, and beautiful chaos.

Join Molière in the Park for the biting, ever-relevant tragicomedy DON JUAN.

A perfect blend of farce and profound ethical questioning, Don Juan remains just as thought-provoking, hilarious, and relevant as when it was first put on (and promptly censored by France’s religious right) some 400 years ago.

About Molière in the Park:
Molière in the Park’s (MIP) mission is to promote empathy and unity within the diverse communities in Brooklyn through the arts. MIP offers free opportunities for all to come together in Prospect Park and experience the benefits of theater. MIP’s vision is of a Brooklyn where all communities can benefit equally from access to theater and the Arts.

For six Tuesdays from July to September, Domino Square transforms into an outdoor cinema playing double billings at 4 pm and 7 pm.
Framed by the unparalleled backdrop of the Manhattan skyline, join us at 4 pm for family-friendly showings with art activities led by A Rosie Day and at 7 pm for main features with drinks from Other Half and snacks from Frita Batido’s and Stretch Pizza.
“Just as things grow fast in movies, I had that familiar conviction that life was beginning over again with the summer” (F. Scott Fitzgerald). After a brutal New York winter, the 2026 Movies in the Square lineup features movies that celebrate the joy of a summer spent outside. With iconic scenes that take place on the hot streets of NYC, classics that transport viewers to Greece and Italy, and an opening movie with a nod to the World Cup, this year’s movies invite us to get outside and enjoy the summer!

Plus, an appearance by Brooklyn Public Library’s Bookmobile.

R, 1 h33m
The Warriors is a cult action thriller set in a dystopian version of New York City, where a street gang is falsely accused of murdering a respected gang leader. Forced to travel from the Bronx back to their home turf in Coney Island, the Warriors must fight their way through hostile gangs across the city while trying to survive the night and clear their name.

Get your blood pumping with this weekly free, fun, and exciting zumba class in Brooklyn Bridge Park! This all-level one hour fitness class is open to all ages and abilities, led by instructors from Dodge YMCA. This is a part of Brooklyn Bridge Park’s Waterfront Workouts series, offering free weekly workouts from May through September.
ZUMBA is a fusion of Latin and International music, utilizing dance themes that create a dynamic, exciting, effective fitness program. The routines feature aerobic training with a combination of fast and slow rhythms that tone and sculpt the body. Note this class takes place on a paved area – sneakers are highly recommended!
Advance registration is highly encouraged but walk-ups welcome as capacity allows. Fitness waivers are required by all participants. View all upcoming dates and full details at brooklynbridgepark.org. Please note holiday weekends are skipped and this event is subject to change due to weather or other extenuating circumstances.
