Flatbush Mending Circle
Thursday, September 10th at 5:00 PM - 7:00 PM
&laquo; Sewing Class Workshops (Williamsburgh Library)
Climate Café &raquo;
Join Flatbush Library for our monthly mending circle! In general, we meet the first Thursday evening of the month from 5-7pm to repair our clothes and meet our neighbors. Basic supplies will be provided, including needles, thread, fabric scraps and a sewing machine. Come ready with an item you wish to repair (a sock to darn, jeans to patch) and start mending.
This program is not intended to be a drop off repair center, but rather a place for folks to come together, sew and learn. All are welcome.

Prospect Park Wellness Walk
Friday, September 11th at 10:00 AM - 12:00 PM
Free
&laquo; Movies Under the Stars (Sep 2026)
Sensory Garden Hour (Greenpoint Library) &raquo;
Join Prospect Park Alliance at the Prospect Park Boathouse for an uplifting and energizing morning walk.
Explore scenic trails along the Lullwater, Peninsula, Midwood or Waterfall. Please note, this walk leaves promptly at 10 am from the Prospect Park Audubon Center.
Prospect Park Wellness Walks will be held on Fridays from April–September from 10 am–12 pm.
Please note that Eventbrite RSVP is required.
Please check your email prior to the event for changes, updates and to confirm the walk has met a minimum participant requirement.
