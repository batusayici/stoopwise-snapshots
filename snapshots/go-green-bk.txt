Molière’s Don Juan (Prospect Park)
Tuesday, September 15th at 11:00 AM - 1:00 PM
&laquo; Community Yoga in Transmitter Park
Yard Sale and Canning Sale (Union St. Garden and Community Development) &raquo;
Join Molière in the Park for the biting, ever-relevant tragicomedy DON JUAN.
Don Juan , one of French literary legend Molière ‘s most controversial works, explores themes of class, sex, religion, and freedom, exposing societal hypocrisy along the way. A perfect blend of farce and profound ethical questioning, Don Juan remains just as thought-provoking, hilarious, and relevant as when it was first put on (and promptly censored by France’s religious right) some 400 years ago.
🎟️ FREE to attend with RSVP
⏱️ Performance runtime: TBD
Directed by Lucie Tiberghien
Translation by Stephen Wadsworth
About Molière in the Park:
Molière in the Park’s (MIP) mission is to promote empathy and unity within the diverse communities in Brooklyn through the arts. MIP offers free opportunities for all to come together in Prospect Park and experience the benefits of theater. MIP’s vision is of a Brooklyn where all communities can benefit equally from access to theater and the Arts.

Event Series
(See All)
Free
&laquo; Keep Calm and Carry Yarn (Greenpoint Library)
Waterfront Walks (Brooklyn Bridge Park) &raquo;
De-stress at the end of the day with a free yoga session in Brooklyn Bridge Park! This all-level one hour fitness class is open to all ages and abilities, led by instructors from Abhaya Yoga. This is a part of Brooklyn Bridge Park’s Waterfront Workouts series, offering free weekly workouts from May through September.
This yoga class integrates postures, breathing exercises, relaxation and meditation to stretch, strengthen and condition.
No equipment is required but participants are encouraged to bring their own yoga mats or towels, if desired, to use on the lawn.
Advance registration is highly encouraged but walk-ups welcome as capacity allows. Fitness waivers are required by all participants. View all upcoming dates and full details at brooklynbridgepark.org. Please note holiday weekends are skipped and this event is subject to change due to weather or other extenuating circumstances.

Wednesday, September 16th at 6:30 AM - 7:30 AM
Free
&laquo; Molière’s Don Juan (Prospect Park)
Weeding Wednesdays at Bushwick Inlet Park &raquo;
Greet the sunrise with a yoga flow.
Every Wednesday morning on the Flex Field, join an invigorating and centering outdoor yoga session. With the sound of the East River in the background and the Manhattan skyline in view, this free class offers a serene start to the day. Please bring a mat.
Mackenzie Rawls: With a background deeply rooted in dance, Mackenzie discovered her passion for teaching early in her career but decided to expand and diversify her offerings. Recognizing the transformative power of yoga, she delved into its principles and philosophy, becoming a certified instructor under the direction of Gabrielle Sulk. In and beyond the studio, Mackenzie has organized workshops, masterclasses, and community events to foster creativity, connection, and growth within her community. Mackenzie’s impact as an educator extends far beyond the physical aspects of dance and yoga. She believes in nurturing the whole person, helping her students develop self-confidence, resilience, and a sense of joy in their practice.
