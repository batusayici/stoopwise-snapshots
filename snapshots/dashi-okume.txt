Dashi Izakaya Autumn Omakase Pop Up
October 24, 2024
Dashi Izakaya Autumn Omakase Pop-Up $85
Shun, the Japanese concept that places the utmost importance on the season. As summer ends and the leaves start to turn, we find ourselves craving the flavors of autumn—salmon, ikura (salmon roe), aromatic mushrooms, sweet kabocha, and crispy lotus root. Autumn is often called 'the season of hearty appetites.
This time, we offer a selection of five appetizers, each featuring autumn ingredients and plenty of dashi and fish from Toyosu fish market. To finish, enjoy a seafood oyakodon topped with salmon from Toyosu and ikura, served over rice cooked in a donabe pot with flavorful dashi. Savor the taste of autumn.
Tasting Menu
Sakiduke- A couple of appetizers using our dashi.
Sashimi - Fresh sashimi from Toyosu fishmarket
Agemono - Deep Fried Dishes of the day using our dashi
Salmon & Ikura Donabe Gohan
House-made Desert.
Sake paring is also available for $60.
Dates & Time
Every Friday and Saturday
One seating from 8pm until the end of November

at Dashi Okume

S
We’re thrilled to announce the return of our Oden Omakase Pop-up, bringing the comforting flavors of Japan’s beloved winter dish to Greenpoint.
In the chilly winter months, there's nothing quite like the warmth that comes from a hearty bowl of oden.
As a dashi specialty store, we've crafted meticulously curated oden, rich with flavorful broth to warm both body and soul.Each item, from the homemade fish cakes to the locally sourced, exquisite vegetables, are prepared with care.To ensure you experience it piping hot, we've incorporated these delights into a thoughtfully designed course menu.
Our commitment extends to appetizers, featuring Toyosu's freshest fish and a finale that highlights the essence of our dashi.Pair it with our curated selection of Sake for a truly delightful experience.

event | UDON OMAKASE POP UP
February 8, 2024
We will be holding a UDON OMAKASE POP UP!
When it gets warmer, Japanese people often crave cold noodles.
In particular, Inaniwa udon from Akita stands out among cold noodles for its 'KOSHI' (elasticity, an important measure of good udon) and 'NODOGOSHI' (smooth texture).
To produce this delicious udon, skilled artisans go through four processes of kneading, twisting, stretching, and drying. Particularly, the technique called "Ayakake," where the dough is stretched thinly by hand, creates an incredibly smooth texture, pleasant mouthfeel, and an unexpected firmness despite its thickness.
Please enjoy this Inaniwa udon with our dipping sauce made with our dashi.
Along with the udon, we've prepared a tasting menu that showcases the rich possibilities of dashi as a dashi store and invites you to indulge in the exquisite taste of the fish from Toyosu in Japan, as we originate from the fish market.
UDON Omakase Course Menu
Sakizuke
Seasonal appetizers using our dashi
Bozushi
Stickstyle Sushi using our fish from Toyosu Fish market
Agemono
Deep Fried Dishes of the day
Inaniwa Udon
Kanmi
House-made dessert
Price $85 per person (excluding tip and tax)
*Sake paring is available for $50

from April 19th
