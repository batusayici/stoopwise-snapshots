Alash Ensemble, Kevin Nathaniel & Dream Seed Live in NYC
Wednesday, October 21, 2026
7:00 PM
9:30 PM
Golden Drum
97 Green St. G1
Brooklyn, NY, 11222
Unites States of America
(map)

ICS
Join us at Golden Drum for a special evening of live music bringing together Alash Ensemble, Kevin Nathaniel, and Dream Seed for an exploration of rhythm, resonance, voice, and music from across cultures.
Alash Ensemble is a trio of internationally recognized master throat singers and instrumentalists from Tuva, a republic in the heart of Central Asia. They are renowned for Tuvan throat singing (khöömei) , an extraordinary vocal tradition in which a singer brings out multiple tones and harmonics within a single voice. Rooted in the musical traditions of Central Asian nomadic culture, Alash honors the music of their homeland while expanding it through influences from jazz, bluegrass, Eastern European folk music, and other contemporary styles.
Kevin Nathaniel and Dream Seed come together for a collaborative performance rooted in rhythm, improvisation, and the transformative possibilities of sound. Kevin Nathaniel is a master percussionist and mbira player whose music draws upon African and diasporic traditions, while Dream Seed creates immersive sound journeys using instruments and musical traditions from around the world. Together, they weave percussion, voice, melody, and meditative sound into a spontaneous musical experience that invites both deep listening and connection.
This concert offers a rare opportunity to experience these artists together in the intimate setting of Golden Drum, moving from the remarkable overtones and ancient vocal traditions of Tuva to the rhythmic and expansive soundscapes of Kevin Nathaniel and Dream Seed.
Come experience an evening of musicianship, cross-cultural musical traditions, improvisation, and the power of sound to bring people together.

About Alash Ensemble
Alash Ensemble are internationally acclaimed masters of Tuvan throat singing (xöömei), a remarkable vocal technique that allows a singer to produce multiple pitches at the same time. Trained in the traditional music of Tuva from childhood, the trio combines extraordinary vocal techniques with indigenous instruments such as the igil, doshpuluur, xomus, and kengirge. While deeply rooted in Tuvan musical heritage, Alash subtly incorporates contemporary influences, including complex harmonies, Western instruments, and modern song forms, creating music that is both innovative and unmistakably Tuvan.
Alash has toured extensively throughout the United States and internationally, earning acclaim from audiences and critics alike; The Washington Post called their music “utterly stunning.” Known for adventurous collaborations, they have performed with artists ranging from the legendary Sun Ra Arkestra to Béla Fleck & the Flecktones, appearing on the Grammy-winning album Jingle All the Way . In addition to performing, Alash is dedicated to teaching and cultural exchange, offering workshops at schools, universities, conservatories, and communities around the world and introducing new generations to the extraordinary musical traditions of Tuva.
https://www.alashensemble.com/
About Kevin Nathaniel
Kevin Nathaniel was born and raised in Detroit, Michigan—Motown and one of America’s great musical centers. Surrounded by extraordinary musicians and music from an early age, his own path as a musician began near the end of college, when encounters with music from Africa inspired him to begin playing, studying, and building instruments.
Kevin’s journey began with the mbira, also known as the kalimba, particularly the mbira traditions of Southern Africa and Zimbabwe. Learning to build and play these instruments opened the door to a lifelong exploration of music and an ever-expanding range of instruments and traditions. The mbira remains at the heart of his musical path, serving as the foundation for his work as a multi-instrumentalist, instrument maker, performer, and musical explorer.
https://www.instagram.com/kevinnathanielhealing
About Dream Seed
Dream Seed is a sound healing collective created by members of Golden Drum, Sacred Arts Research Foundation, and Didge Project. As students of Maestro Manuel Rufino, the members of Dream Seed are dedicated to the study and preservation of the sacred traditions of humanity and the continued exploration of sound as a healing modality of the Aquarian Age. Dream Seed studies and works with indigenous and traditional elders, continuously seeking to expand their knowledge of sound and healing as universal methods of cultivating harmony, peace, and well-being.
Members of Dream Seed include Brooke Gillespie, AJ Block, Jerry Walsh, Hannah Apollonia, Naku Kiwanuka, Matt Canale, Xango Shola, Mateusz Bazgier, Nicole Casanova, El Stone, Michael Fisher & Ixchel Prisma.
More info on Dream Seed at http://dreamseedsound.com/

ARA Healing Arts School Year Long Apprenticeship & Launch
Tuesday, November 3, 2026
7:00 PM
Tuesday, October 26, 2027
8:00 PM
Golden Drum
97 Green St. G1
Brooklyn, NY, 11222
Unites States of America
(map)

ICS
Golden Drum is pleased to announce the launch of ARA Healing Arts School , guided by community members Brooke Hamre Gillespie and Nicole Adriana Casanova . ARA Healing Arts honors and integrates the sacred traditions of humanity that support awakening, consciousness, and holistic healing. Brooke and Nicole bring over twenty years each of dedicated study and practice in traditional healing arts and spiritual lineages.
This year-long, 150-hour training program, beginning in September 2026, supports personal healing and awakening, while also addressing interpersonal and planetary healing across all levels of relationship—physical, emotional, mental, and spiritual. The course is designed to cultivate awareness, balance, and responsibility in how we relate to ourselves, others, and the world.
Sign up for an enrollment interview here
ARA Healing Arts School Apprenticeship is open to participants of all experience levels. Throughout the year, students will be introduced to a wide range of healing modalities and practices, including breath, dance, sound, sacred art, lightwork, astrology, yoga, Reiki, tarot, earth-based traditions, elemental work, Ayurveda, and other complementary healing techniques. Our training also illuminates friendship and good relationships as crucial for ongoing growth and well-being in our lives.
This extended format allows for a deep and holistic exploration of the healing arts, providing sustained support for each participant’s inner journey. Unlike short-term or weekend-based training, this year-long container offers the time, continuity, and personal guidance necessary to develop a grounded, foundational, and integrated healing practice. The program is intentionally designed to support long-term growth, self-awareness, and embodiment, fostering expanded consciousness, heightened sensitivity, and open-hearted presence.
The first year of ARA Healing Arts School focuses on self-discovery as the foundation of healing work. Participants will explore energetic anatomy, including the chakras and subtle bodies, as well as sound, frequency, astrology, embodiment practices, energetic hygiene, and the unique modalities that resonate with each individual’s healing path. As the year progresses, participants will also begin learning how to work with others, with an emphasis on ethical practice, self-awareness, and continual personal healing. Guest teachers will be invited throughout the year to share their specialized knowledge and lived experience with participants.
In addition, students will have the opportunity to participate in two immersive retreats: one held in Upstate New York and a second longer retreat offered at a location to be determined. These retreats provide an opportunity to step away from the rhythms of daily life and enter a dedicated learning environment where participants can deepen their practice, strengthen community connections, engage in ceremony, and experience extended periods of hands-on training, reflection, and personal transformation in a supportive and nature-based setting. Retreat information will be given upon registration in the course.
Through a combination of online and in-person workshops, activations, and experiential learning, participants are guided through a heart-centered journey designed to bring the mind, body, heart, and spirit into deeper alignment—with the self, the Earth, the cosmos, and all relations.
To join this life-affirming apprenticeship program FILL OUT THIS FORM and Brooke and Nicole will set up an interview with you.

This artistic tradition was a brand new experience to all the participants and we learned so much in four hours and all now have gorgeous pieces of art to show for it. Raquy Danziger is an internationally acclaimed musician, artist, teacher and composer. Considered to be one of the foremost experts and virtuosos on the oriental goblet drum known as the darbuka, Raquy is renowned for her electrifying performances. Through her mission to introduce the darbuka to people all over the world, Raquy has inspired a new generation of players.

“Djembe” means coming together and going together in peace. It’s an instrument that brings together our hearts when we all play together.
We are grateful to have traditional Djembe player, Abdoul Doumbia of Mali coming to teach us about the traditional healing rhythms and history of the Djembe. These classes are for all levels of players, there is an abundance of knowledge being shared and regardless of your experience, this is a special and rare opportunity to learn from a master.
*We will have some drums for use, but if you have a djembe we recommend bringing it. Thank you!

Register Now
Abdoul Doumbia was born in Bamako, Mali in 1965 and was raised in the village of Foutaka Zambougou near the ancient Bamana city of Segou. At the age of five Abdoul began to learn the music of his culture. He went on to complete a 16 year apprenticeship with master drummer Moriba Keita in Bamako. Abdoul worked with many professional drum and dance companies in Mali, including nine years as lead drummer for the renowned traditional drum and dance troupe Babemba, and was chosen to represent his region for eight consecutive years in the National Drum Festival of Mali. Abdoul immigrated to the United States in 1991 to accept a position at Brown University. He taught regularly on the east coast, including classes at Yale, Harvard, Princeton, RISDI, the University of Florida, and the University of Connecticut before moving to Boulder, Colorado in 1998 where he currently lives and teaches. In Colorado Abdoul has taught classes at CU, Naropa and The Colorado College. Abdoul is known for his mastery of Malian djembe and dunun and is a respected source of information regarding Mali's rich culture and history.
